# AI

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Is an AI deciding my politics?**
:::

What the AI is allowed to do, what it is never allowed to decide, and the hard rules the build itself enforces.

## What this area covers

- AI as a verifier of reasoning rather than an oracle.
- Checking a chain from first principles.
- Assessing transcripts against a published rubric.
- The advisory-only pass that never awards a point.
- What a human must always sign off on.
- The hard rules, including the one that says this codebase never downloads media, enforced by a test that fails the build..
