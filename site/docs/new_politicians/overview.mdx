# Good New Leaders

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Who should I vote FOR, not just against?**
:::

The people worth electing who have never been in office — assessed on what they have said and done, not on a voting record they do not have.

## What this area covers

- The roster of new political leaders.
- How somebody gets on it and who may recommend them (anyone, including a logged-out visitor).
- What is assessed — interviews, writing, public commitments, background.
- The percent-known companion.
- Worrisome challengers and why flagging one costs sources..

## What it deliberately leaves to other areas

- Anyone with a voting record — that is legacy_politicians..
